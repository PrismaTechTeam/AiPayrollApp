# AiPayroll Mobile App – User Manual

For employees and HR staff. The app shows on your phone as **Payroll App**; inside, it is called **AiPayroll**.

All dates and times in the app are Malaysia time. Money is shown as, for example, **RM 1,234.50**.

---

## 1. Getting started

### 1.1 Install and sign in

1. Install the app from the link or store your HR gives you, then open it.
2. On **Welcome back**, enter your **Email** and **Password**. It is the same email and password you use for AiPayroll on the web.
3. Leave **Keep me signed in** ticked if this is your own phone. If you untick it, you must sign in again the next time the app starts.
4. Tap **Sign in**.

If the app says "Email or password is incorrect", check both and try again. If you sign in to the AiPayroll website with Google, you have no app password yet: tap **Forgot password?** to set one.

### 1.2 Create an account (new employees)

1. On the sign-in screen, tap **Create account** (under "New here?").
2. Enter your **Full name** (as on your IC or passport), **Email**, **Password** (at least 6 characters) and **Confirm password**.
3. Tap **Continue**.
4. On **Check your inbox**, open the verification email (you can tap **Open Mail**) and tap the link inside.
5. Come back to the app and tap **Continue**. If the email did not arrive, tap **Resend link**.

### 1.3 Forgot password

1. On the sign-in screen, tap **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email and tap **Send reset link**.
3. Open the email and set a new password. If it does not arrive, check your spam folder. **Use a different email** lets you try another address.

### 1.4 Join your company

After your first sign-in you see **No company yet**.

1. Tap **Join a company**.
2. Type the **Invitation code** from HR, or tap **Scan QR code** and point the camera at HR's QR code. (Allow the camera when asked.)
3. Optionally enter your **Employee number**.
4. Tap **Send request**.
5. The **Join request** page shows **Waiting for HR**. It checks for HR's decision by itself. You can tap **Cancel request** if you sent it to the wrong company.
6. When HR approves, tap **Open company**. If HR does not approve, the page shows **Not approved** and HR's reason.

**Your join requests** lists every company you asked to join.

### 1.5 Switch company

If you belong to more than one company:

1. On Home, tap the company name at the top left.
2. Under **Your companies**, tap the company you want. The whole app switches to it.
3. **Manage companies** opens the **Companies** page. The current company has a blue tick. From there you can also **Join another company** and see **Your join requests**.

You can also reach **Companies** from **Profile** (tap the company under **Company**).

## 2. For employees

### 2.1 Home and the bottom bar

The bottom bar has **Home · Requests · Punch · Leave · Profile**. Punch is the round button in the middle.

Home shows:
- The company name (tap to switch) and the bell for **Notifications**. A red dot means something is unread.
- A greeting and today's date.
- Tiles for **My Requests**, **My Leaves**, **My Payslip**, **My Documents**, **My Claims** and **My Attendance**, each with a short status (for example, how many are pending, or leave days left).
- **Recent activity**: your requests, leave, claims and payslips in one list.
- **View all** opens **All services**, where you can also search (for example "payslip", "leave", "punch"). **My Training** is found here.

Pull down on Home to refresh.

If Home says **No employee record here**, your account is not yet linked to your employee record. Ask HR to link it.

### 2.2 Punch in and out

1. Tap **Punch** in the bottom bar.
2. Check the choice at the top: **Clock in** or **Clock out** (it says **Break in** after a break).
3. The card shows your location. If your company set work locations, you must be inside one. The card tells you how far you are and which location it is before you tap.
4. Tap the big button (for example **Tap to clock in**).
5. Confirm with your phone's fingerprint or face check. If your phone has none, the app takes a photo instead: look at the camera and keep your face inside the outline.
6. You see **Clocked in** or **Clocked out** with the time.

Notes:
- If the page says **Your company records attendance on the office device**, clock in on the fingerprint or face terminal. Punches from the phone would not count.
- If location is off or blocked, tap **Allow location** or **Open settings**.
- If the app detects a fake ("mock") location, it will not record the punch. Turn mock location off.

### 2.3 My Attendance

1. On Home, tap **My Attendance**.
2. Use the arrows to move between months. Each day shows your punches and hours, with totals for Worked, Present, Late and Absent.
3. If a month says **This month has been finalised by HR**, it can no longer be changed from the app.

### 2.4 Forgot to punch (missed punch request)

1. Open **My Attendance**.
2. On the day with a missing punch, tap **Ask HR** (or tap **Missed a punch? Ask HR** at the top, then **Report a missed punch**).
3. Choose what you missed (**Clock in**, **Clock out**, **Break out** or **Break in**), the **Date**, the **Time** and a **Reason**.
4. Tap **Send to HR**.

Your attendance updates once HR approves it. To see your requests, open **Missed a punch? Ask HR** (the **Punch Requests** page). While a request is still pending you can **Withdraw** it.

### 2.5 Leave

**Apply for leave**
1. Tap **Leave** in the bottom bar (or **My Leaves** on Home).
2. Tap **Apply for Leave**.
3. Choose the **Leave type**, then the **First day** and **Last day**. If you need part of a day, choose **Half day only** (Morning or Afternoon), **First day is a half day**, **Last day is a half day**, or **Just a few hours**.
4. Attach a supporting document if needed (for example a medical certificate) with **Add a file**.
5. Add a reason (optional, but it saves your approver asking).
6. Send. You see **Sent for approval**, and you get a notification when it is decided.

**Check your leave**
- **My Leaves** shows how many days of each type you have left and your recent applications.
- Tap a leave type to see Entitled, Carried, Used and Pending days, and **Apply for this leave**.
- **View all** opens **Leave History** for the whole year.
- Tap an application to see its status and who has approved it so far.

**Withdraw or cancel**
- While an application is pending, open it and tap **Withdraw this leave**. The days go back to your balance.
- For approved leave that has not ended, open it and tap **Cancel this leave**. The days go back to your balance and your approver sees it was cancelled.

### 2.6 Claims

**Make a claim**
1. On Home, tap **My Claims**, then **New claim** (or **Make a claim**).
2. Choose the **Claim type**, enter the **Amount** in RM and **When you paid**.
3. Under **What was it for?**, describe the expense.
4. Attach the receipt: **Take a photo**, **Choose a photo** or **Choose a PDF**. Some claim types require a receipt. The receipt cannot be added after sending.
5. Add the **Receipt number** if the receipt has one.
6. Tap **Send claim**.

If the claim is over your limit, the app warns you (**Over your limit**). You can **Go back** or **Send anyway**.

**Track your claims**
- **My Claims** lists your claims with their status. **What can I claim?** shows the claim types and limits.
- Tap a claim to see the receipt and its **History**. While it is waiting for approval you can **Edit** it or **Withdraw** it.

### 2.7 Requests (letters and other HR requests)

1. Tap **Requests** in the bottom bar (or **My Requests** on Home).
2. Tap **New request**.
3. Choose the **Request type**, write a **Note for HR** (what you need, and why) and add files if needed (**Add a file**).
4. Tap **Send request**.

To follow up:
- **My Requests** lists your requests. Use the chips **All**, **Pending**, **Approved**, **Rejected**, **Cancelled**. "HR replied" shows when HR has written back.
- Tap a request to see HR's messages and files, and the **Timeline**.
- Tap **Reply to HR** to answer HR.
- While it is still waiting, you can tap **Cancel request**.

### 2.8 Payslips

1. On Home, tap **My Payslip**.
2. The latest payslip shows your net pay. Tap a month to open the full payslip (income, deductions, overtime, leave, employer contributions and summary).
3. Tap **Save PDF** to save or share it, or **Print**.

A payslip appears once your company completes the payroll run for that month. **Show more payslips** shows earlier months.

### 2.9 My Documents

1. On Home, tap **My Documents**.
2. Documents that need you are at the top, marked **Not sent**, **Sent back**, **Expired** or **Requested**. Others show **Checking** (HR is looking at it), **Provided**, **Expiring**, **Waived** or **Optional**.
3. Tap a document, then **Send this document** (or **Send a new copy** / **Send the renewed copy**) and choose the file.
4. Some documents have **Get the blank form** to download first, and **Download my copy** for ones already on file.

After you send a document it shows **Checking** until HR looks at it.

### 2.10 My Training

1. On Home, tap **View all**, then **My Training**.
2. Courses are marked **Not done**, **Expired**, **Due soon**, **Expiring**, **Done**, **Excused** or **Optional**.
3. Tap a course and **Open attendance record** to view the record or certificate.

### 2.11 Notifications

1. Tap the bell at the top right of Home.
2. Tap a notification to open the leave, claim or request it is about. Back returns you to the same place in the list.
3. The tick icon at the top right marks everything as read (it only shows when something is unread).
4. **Clear all notifications** at the bottom removes them all, after you confirm.

If a phone notification belongs to another of your companies, the app asks **Open in (company)?**. Tap **Switch and open** to switch company and open it, or **Stay here**.

### 2.12 Profile, password, two-factor, devices

Tap **Profile** in the bottom bar.

- **Your name and photo**: tap the top card to open **Edit Profile**. Change or remove your photo, edit **First Name** and **Last Name**, and tap **Save Name**. Payslips use the name HR has on file.
- **Company**: shows the company you are in. Tap it to open **Companies**.
- **Change Password**: enter the **Current Password**, then the **New Password** twice, and tap **Update Password**. The app then asks whether to **Sign out everywhere** (every phone and browser, including this one) or **Not now**.
- **Two-Factor Authentication**: tap **Set Up Two-Factor**, then **Add to Authenticator App** (or **Copy key**, or **Show QR code for another phone**). Enter the 6-digit code and tap **Verify & Turn On**. This applies when you sign in on the **web**. To turn it off, use **Turn Off Two-Factor** and enter a current code.
- **Devices**: the phones that get notifications. Tap the bell on a row to stop notifications to that phone. **Sign out of all devices** signs out every phone and browser, this one included.
- **Help**, **About** (with the **Privacy Policy**) and **Sign Out**.

## 3. For HR and approvers

### 3.1 Who sees the HR view

What you see depends on the rights your company gives your role on the web:

| Right | What it unlocks in the app |
|---|---|
| Leave approval | **Leave Approval** |
| Request approval | **Request Approval** |
| Claim approval | **Claim Approval** and **Claim Types** |
| Request type view | **Request Types** |
| Attendance punch report or work card view | **Team today** on Home, **Team Today** list and map |
| Attendance work card view | **Punch requests** row and **Punch Approval** list (view only) |
| Employee portal view | **Invite Employees** |

Each item only appears if you have its right. When rights change on the web, pull down on Home to refresh. The app also refreshes them by itself after it has been in the background for 5 minutes.

HR **without** an employee record (for example the company owner) has no punch, leave or payslip of their own. HR **with** an employee record also has every employee function in Section 2.

### 3.2 HR Home

From the top:
1. Company name (tap to switch) and the bell.
2. Greeting and today's date.
3. **To decide**: one row for each approval you are allowed to give: **Leave**, **Requests**, **Claims** (and **Punch requests** if you have the attendance work card right). Each shows how many are waiting. Tap a row to open that list. A grey bar means it is still loading; red **Couldn't load** means that count failed.
4. **Team today** (with an attendance right): how many have **punched in** and how many have **not punched in**. "Not punched in" includes people on leave or on a rest day. When the card can be opened, tap it to see **Team Today**.
5. **Waiting for you**: the newest pending leave, requests and claims together: who, what and how long it has waited. Tap one to open it and decide. When nothing is pending it says **Nothing is waiting for you**.
6. If you are also an employee, **My work** tiles and **Recent activity** follow.

If something fails to load, a red row **Some figures could not load · Try again** appears. Tap it or pull down to refresh.

**Bottom bar**
- HR without an employee record: **Home · Requests · Claims · Leave · Profile**. Requests, Claims and Leave open the approval lists (each tab only shows if you have that right).
- HR with an employee record: **Home · Requests · Punch · Leave · Profile**, the same as employees. Reach the approval lists from Home or **All services**.

**All services** (tap **View all** on Home) groups everything you can open: **Approvals** (Request, Leave, Claim Approval), **Setup** (Request Types, Claim Types, Invite Employees), **Team** (Team Today, Punch Approval), **My work** (if you are also an employee) and **Account** (Settings, Companies, Notifications, Help). Use the search box to find a page by name.

### 3.3 Approval lists – how they work

**Leave Approval**, **Request Approval** and **Claim Approval** all work the same way:

1. The list opens on **Pending**. Other chips show decided items: **Approved**, **Rejected**, **All** (Leave Approval also has **Cancelled**).
2. Each card shows the person (initials, name, employee code), the type with a coloured dot, the dates or amount, the reason, and a grey line such as "Applied 4 Sep · waiting 34 days".
3. Tap **Approve** on the card. A confirmation shows the details. Tap **Approve** again.
4. Or tap **Reject**. You must give a reason. The employee sees it.
5. The card leaves the list and a short message confirms the decision (for example "Approved · RM 40.00 · Aisyah").
6. Tap anywhere else on a card to open its **details** page, with **Reject** and **Approve** at the bottom.

Rules:
- **Nobody approves their own item.** If you are also an employee, your own items show "(you)" and have no buttons. Another approver must decide them.
- **Approve on behalf.** If a step is waiting for another approver, HR can approve it. The confirmation names who it is waiting on.
- If someone else decided the item first, an error appears and the list reloads.

### 3.4 Leave Approval

1. Open **Leave** under **To decide** on Home (or the **Leave** tab, or **All services > Leave Approval**).
2. Leave starting soonest is at the top. If the leave has already started or the dates have passed, the grey line starts with **Started (date)** or **Dates passed** in red.
3. The grey line also says who the leave is waiting on, for example "with Ahmad (step 1 of 2)". A paperclip means a file is attached.
4. After you approve, the message says **Approved – (name)** or, if there is another step, **Step approved – now with (next approver)**.

**Leave Details** shows the person, type, dates and status, then **Applied**, **Balance** (left for that leave type), **Also off** (others in the same department off on those dates), **Waiting on**, the **Attachment** (tap to open), the **Reason** and the full **Approval** trail.

**Cancel approved leave.** For an employee's approved leave that has not ended, HR with leave approval rights sees **Cancel leave** at the bottom. Enter a reason; the days go back to the employee's balance and they see your reason.

**Department approvers.** Employees whom HR made a department approver see **Department leave** under **To decide** on Home. It opens Leave Approval with only the leave waiting for them. They decide only their department's steps, never their own leave.

### 3.5 Request Approval

1. Open **Requests** under **To decide** (or the **Requests** tab, or **All services > Request Approval**).
2. **Approve** asks, for example, "Approve Ahmad's Equipment request?". **Reject** asks for a **Reason for rejection**.
3. **Request Details** shows the person, type and note, then **Submitted**, **Waiting** and **Files**, then **Messages**, files and the **Timeline**.
4. To answer without deciding, tap **Reply to the employee** (or **Edit reply**). Type your message and tap **Send**. The employee sees it on their phone.
5. To send a document back, tap **Send a file back**.
6. If you are also an employee, **Mine** at the top right opens your own requests.

**Request Types** (in **All services > Setup**):
- Tap a type (or its pencil) to edit it. Tap **+** to add one (**Short code**, **Name employees see**, **Category (optional)**), then **Save**.
- Switch off **Offered to employees** to stop offering a type. It then shows an **Off** tag.
- A type that has been used cannot be deleted; the app offers **Turn it off** instead.
- What you can do here depends on your create, edit and delete rights.

### 3.6 Claim Approval

1. Open **Claims** under **To decide** (or the **Claims** tab, or **All services > Claim Approval**).
2. The card shows the amount and the date it was spent, and "Receipt attached" when there is one.
3. **Approve** asks you to confirm. **Reject** requires a reason.
4. **Claim Details** shows the person, type, amount, spend date, **What it was for**, the **Receipt** (tap to view full screen; use the share icon to save it), receipt number and date, and **History**.
5. If your role can approve claims but not view decided ones, the Approved, Rejected and All chips show **Past claims are not open to you** with **Back to Pending**.

**Claim Types** (in **All services > Setup**) lists the active claim types and their limits. Edit types and limits on the web dashboard.

### 3.7 Team attendance: Team Today, map and punch requests

**Team Today** (from Home's **Team today** card or **All services > Team Today**)
1. The list shows every active employee with a badge: green **In** (time), grey **Out** (time), **On leave**, or amber **Not yet**.
2. Use the chips to filter: **All**, **Not yet**, **In**, plus **Out** and **On leave** when anyone is in those groups.
3. Search by name, role or department. Pull down to refresh.
4. Rows with a map marker have a clock-in location. Tap one to see it on the map. Fingerprint-terminal clock-ins have a time but no place.

**Clock-in Map**
1. Tap the map button at the top right of Team Today to see everyone with a location at once.
2. Tap a pin to see who clocked in there and when (and when they left, if they have). If several people clocked in at the same spot, their names show under **Same place**.
3. The map never asks for your own location.

**Punch Approval** (forgotten punches)
- With the attendance work card right, Home shows **Punch requests** under **To decide**, and **All services > Team** has **Punch Approval**.
- It lists everyone's missed-punch requests under **Pending**, **Approved** and **Rejected**.
- **Deciding is done on the web for now**: pending items show **Decide on the web**. On the web go to **Attendance > Daily > Punch Requests**.

If you lack the right, these pages show **No access to team attendance** or **No access to punch requests**. Use the back arrow.

### 3.8 Invite employees

1. Open **Profile > Invite employees** or **All services > Invite Employees** (needs the employee portal right).
2. The page shows the company's invitation code and QR code. Tap **Copy** or **Share** to send it to new staff.
3. If it says **No code yet** or **Expired**, create a new code on the web (Employee Portal).

New staff then follow Section 1.4. Approve their join request on the web.

### 3.9 What is web-only

- Deciding forgotten-punch requests: **Attendance > Daily > Punch Requests**.
- Editing claim types and limits.
- Creating or renewing the invitation code (Employee Portal).
- Approving join requests from new staff.
- Linking a user account to an employee record.

## 4. Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Email or password is incorrect" | Use the same login as the AiPayroll website. If you use Google on the web, tap **Forgot password?** to set a password. |
| No verification email | Check spam, then tap **Resend link** on **Check your inbox**. |
| "No employee record here", or no Punch / Leave | Your account is not linked to an employee record in this company. Ask HR to link it. |
| Punch button is grey | Read the card above it: allow location, move closer to the work location, turn off mock location, or use the office device if your company requires it. |
| Forgot to punch | **My Attendance > Ask HR** (Section 2.4). |
| HR sees "This account can't approve leave on the phone yet" | Some HR accounts without an employee record cannot decide leave on the phone yet. Decide it on the web (**Leave > Applications**). Until the app restarts, cards show **Decide this on the web for now**. Attachments for those accounts also open only on the web. |
| An approval list or count is missing | You do not have that right in this company. Ask your administrator, then pull down on Home to refresh. |
| "Some figures could not load" or "Couldn't load" | Check your connection and tap **Try again** or pull down to refresh. |
| Notifications is empty | Waiting approvals are counted on Home under **To decide**. Notifications shows approvals, replies and reminders as they arrive. |
| Payslip, attendance or leave balance looks wrong | The app shows what your company recorded. Ask HR or payroll to correct it. |
| Lost phone | On another phone, sign in and go to **Profile > Change Password**, then choose **Sign out everywhere**. **Profile > Devices > Sign out of all devices** does the same. |
| Wrong company showing | Tap the company name on Home and choose the right one. |
